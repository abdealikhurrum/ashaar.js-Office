Kashida fitting — live, on the real engine

Each couplet line is fit to the dashed column width using the actual add-in modules (AshaarJustify.justifyRuns, AshaarTatweel, AshaarKashidaFontswap) and your real fonts. Mehr fills by inserting tatweels only at its Beta-2.0 whitelisted joins; Jameel fills by swapping whole connected segments (fasl) to the wider Kasheeda face. Both then close any remaining gap with capped inter-word micro-spacing (the hybrid fill), and accept a slightly short line if the cap is hit. Drag the slider to watch the fit adapt.
